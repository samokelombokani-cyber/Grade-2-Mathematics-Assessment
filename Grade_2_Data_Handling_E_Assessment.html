Grade 2 Mathematics: Our Favourite Fruits

Formative e-assessment | Data Handling | Total: 50 marks | Suggested time: 20–25 minutes

Hello mathematician! A class of learners voted for their favourite fruit. Study the table and pictograph, then answer the questions.

Remember: read the title, categories and key. Count each picture carefully.

1. Look at the class survey. Which fruit was chosen by the most learners? (4 marks)

2. How many learners chose apples? (4 marks)

3. Which fruit was chosen by the fewest learners? (4 marks)

4. How many learners took part in the survey altogether? (4 marks)

5. How many more learners chose bananas than grapes? (4 marks)

6. A pictograph uses pictures or symbols to show information. (4 marks)

7. If one picture stands for one learner, how many banana pictures should appear? (4 marks)

8. Which title best describes this information? (4 marks)

9. If two more learners choose oranges, how many learners would choose oranges? (4 marks)

10. Write one sentence describing something you notice about the survey. (14 marks)

Teacher-marked: award up to 6 marks for a clear, accurate observation using the data.

Questions 1–9 are automatically checked (36 marks). Question 10 is marked by your teacher (14 marks).
